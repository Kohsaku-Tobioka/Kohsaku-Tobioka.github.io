Posted a new paper with colleagues at Osaka University: Takaya Iwai, Prof. Ryosuke Sato, and Takumu Yamanaka.  
[[arXiv:2602.15117]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15117)  To be published in JHEP

A QCD axion with a mass near 10 MeV that couples to the up quark, down quark, and electron was proposed as a viable possibility. In this paper, we reexamine it with kaon decays.
1. Analyze the K_L → π0 π0 e+ e- measurement of the KTeV experiment in detail.
2. Reinterpret the K+ decay measurements at E949 and NA62 for the diphoton and effectively invisible decay modes of the axion.
3. Find that the viable window is excluded, mainly by the KTeV bound, even after accounting for the uncertainties of the chiral Lagrangian.
